---
title: "Report findings"
description: "How to report a bug or finding from the soak so the team can act on it"
---

Every finding goes through the **Feedback** page in the app. No sign-in is needed, and each report reaches the
team's review queue straight away. The form itself is described in
[Report a bug or finding](/v3/guides/report-a-bug).

## What to include

| Field | Why it helps |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | one line we can recognize: "Refund still shows pending after cancel" |
| **Category** and **Severity** | so the right person looks first. **Blocker** means you couldn't finish. |
| **What were you trying to do?** | the goal, in your words |
| **What actually happened?** | include any error message, exactly as shown |
| **What did you expect instead?** | |
| **Steps to reproduce** | in order, as far as you remember |
| **Transaction Details** | the trade ID from the trade page, any transaction hash, the chains and token |
| Screenshots or a screen recording | the fastest way for us to see it |
| Your device, browser and wallet | many issues are wallet- or phone-specific |

<Warning>
Never include private keys or seed phrases, even test ones.
</Warning>

## Picking a severity

| Severity | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| **Blocker** | you couldn't complete the flow at all, or funds didn't arrive |
| **High** | it worked, but only after a workaround, or something was wrong |
| **Low** | cosmetic, wording, or a small annoyance |

## After you submit

You'll see **Feedback logged!** with a reference such as `PF-30001`. Quote it if you follow up with the team.

To follow up, or to discuss a finding, post in the [ProofBridge Discord](https://discord.gg/vHTuw8prT7) with your
reference.
