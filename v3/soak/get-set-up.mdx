Work through this once. Each step links the full guide if you get stuck.

## 1. Wallets

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Ethereum Sepolia" icon="/logos/ethereum.svg">
    MetaMask, Rainbow or another browser wallet on desktop. On a phone, use WalletConnect.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stellar Testnet" icon="/logos/stellar.svg">
    Freighter, xBull, Lobstr or Hana on desktop. On a phone, use xBull or WalletConnect.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Switch each wallet to its testnet: **Sepolia** in your EVM wallet, **Testnet** in your Stellar wallet.

Open the app from the **Soak** tab on [The soak](/v3/soak) page, click **Connect Wallet**, and sign in on each
network. Connecting the second wallet links it to the same account. [Connect a wallet](/v3/guides/connect-wallet).

## 2. Test funds

| You need | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Sepolia ETH, for gas | any public Sepolia faucet |
| Testnet XLM, for fees | Stellar Friendbot (it also creates a new account) |
| The bridge tokens (WXLM and PB on Sepolia, wETH and PB on Stellar) | the in-app **Faucet**: 1,000,000 per claim |

[Get test tokens](/v3/guides/faucet).

## 3. Your settlement key

Every wallet you trade from needs a settlement key, registered once. On the **BLS Keys** page, click **Register**,
sign the derivation message, then sign the registration. The relayer pays the gas. Do this for each wallet.
[Register a settlement key](/v3/guides/register-a-settlement-key).

While you're there, **file an emergency stop** for your key: it's part of what we're testing.

## Ready check

- [ ] Signed in on Sepolia and Stellar, both wallets linked
- [ ] Sepolia ETH and testnet XLM in the wallets
- [ ] Bridge tokens from the faucet
- [ ] **BLS Keys** shows **Registered** for each wallet

Then go to [What to test](/v3/soak/what-to-test).
