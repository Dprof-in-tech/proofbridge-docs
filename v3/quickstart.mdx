## Before you start

- **Wallets:** an EVM wallet on Sepolia and/or a Stellar wallet on
  Testnet. You need the wallet of the chain you're depositing from. See
  [Connect a wallet](/v3/guides/connect-wallet).
- **Test funds:** Sepolia ETH for gas and XLM on Stellar Testnet. The
  in-app **Faucet** covers the bridge tokens ([Get test tokens](/v3/guides/faucet)).

## Step 1: Register your settlement key (once per wallet)

Every trading wallet needs a settlement key registered before it can
complete a trade. On the **BLS Keys** page:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Derive">
    Sign the derivation message. Your key is computed from this signature,
    so you can regenerate it on any device by signing again. There is
    nothing to back up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    Sign the registration. It carries a proof of possession, so nobody can
    register a key they don't hold.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The relayer submits the registration to the registries on **both**
    chains and pays the gas. The page shows the key as registered once
    both confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Both wallets registered, each on its own network">
  <img src="/images/v3/keys-02-registered.png" alt="The BLS Keys page with two ready keys, each row showing its address, key, network and a Registered status alongside Revoke" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
Keys belong to **wallets**, not accounts. If you trade from two wallets
(one per chain), each wallet registers its own key.
</Warning>

## Step 2: Open an order

On the **Bridge** page, pick the chains and token, then choose an ad.
Enter the amount; the app shows what you pay, the
[fee](/v3/concepts/fees) and what you receive. Set the receive address on
the destination chain, then **Hold to Bridge**.

Your wallet then runs the deposit:

1. **Approve in Wallet:** a one-time token approval (token routes only)
2. **Submit On-Chain:** the deposit into the OrderPortal escrow
3. **Finalize Deposit:** the app tells the relayer it landed

<Frame caption="Amount, fee and what you'll get">
  <img src="/images/v3/bridge-02-amount.png" alt="The Bridge dialog with an amount entered, showing You Pay, the transaction fee and You'll Get, above the Hold to Bridge button" />
</Frame>

If you close the wallet popup by mistake, the order waits for you on the
**Orders** page: **Complete Order** serves the same transaction without reserving
more of the maker's liquidity. Unfunded orders are released after ~30
minutes.

## Step 3: Co-sign

When both deposits are confirmed, the trade page shows **Your Turn**.
Click **Co-sign Settlement**: your settlement key signs the trade in the app, with no
transaction and no gas. The maker signs their half independently, or
their agent does.

<Frame caption="Co-sign: instant, no gas">
  <img src="/images/v3/bridge-03-cosign.png" alt="A locked trade with Sign with BLS Key as the active step, a Co-sign Settlement button, and the timeline showing the maker locked funds" />
</Frame>

## Step 4: Watch it settle

Nothing else to do. The steps advance as the signatures are combined, the
proofs are built and the payouts are submitted:

```
Sign with BLS Key → Aggregated → Submit On-chain → Completed
```

Your tokens arrive at your receive address on the destination chain. The
trade page's timeline links every transaction.

<Note>
If the trade doesn't settle by its deadline, it doesn't get stuck: it can
be cancelled and your deposit refunded. See
[Cancels and refunds](/v3/concepts/cancels-and-refunds).
</Note>
