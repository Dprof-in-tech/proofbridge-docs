ProofBridge is a peer-to-peer cross-chain bridge. Two counterparties trade
directly, a **maker** (liquidity provider) and a **bridger**, and each
side's funds are escrowed in a contract on its own chain. Today it runs
between Ethereum and Stellar.

Three things decide every trade:

- **The two traders authorize it.** Both parties sign the settlement with
  their BLS settlement keys. The contracts on each chain check the
  combined signature against an on-chain key registry. No trade needs
  ProofBridge's permission.
- **Deposits are proven, not trusted.** A payout on one chain carries a
  zero-knowledge proof that the other side's deposit really is recorded on
  the other chain. The destination chain can't read the source chain; the
  proof carries that fact across.
- **Every trade has an ending.** Each order has a deadline. If a trade
  doesn't settle by then, it can be cancelled and the deposit refunded, or
  either party can file a dispute. These paths are part of the contracts,
  not a support process.

The relayer coordinates: it collects the two signatures, builds the
proofs and submits the payouts. It holds no funds and grants no
approvals. The data it submits is public, so anyone can submit the same
transactions.

## What's new in v3

- **Fees:** 0.3% to the maker and 0.2% to the protocol on each trade
  ([Fees](/v3/concepts/fees)).
- **Deadlines, cancels and refunds:** every order ends, one way or
  another ([Deadlines and clocks](/v3/concepts/deadlines-and-clocks),
  [Cancels and refunds](/v3/concepts/cancels-and-refunds)).
- **Disputes:** either party can file, with evidence
  ([Disputes](/v3/concepts/disputes)).
- **Your own trade secret:** each party derives its half of the secret
  that unlocks its payout ([Trade secrets](/v3/concepts/trade-secrets)).
- **Agents:** an agent can lock orders on your ads, within an on-chain
  policy you set ([Agents](/v3/concepts/agents)).
