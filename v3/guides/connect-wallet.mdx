ProofBridge works with one wallet per chain:

- **EVM** (Sepolia): MetaMask, Rainbow and other browser wallets, or
  WalletConnect
- **Stellar** (Testnet): xBull, Freighter, Lobstr, Hana, or WalletConnect

Click **Connect Wallet** to open the wallet hub. It lists each network
and whether you're connected and signed in there.

## On a phone

Freighter, Lobstr and Hana's browser versions are desktop extensions. On a
phone, connect with **xBull** or through **WalletConnect** (for example,
the Lobstr or Freighter mobile app).

## Which wallets do you need?

The wallet of the chain **you deposit from** is required. Connecting both
is recommended:

- as a **Bridger**, you deposit on the order chain and receive on the
  other chain
- as a **Maker**, your liquidity lives on the ad chain and you receive on
  the order chain

## Sign in

Connecting asks for a one-time sign-in signature: a challenge, with no
transaction and no gas. EVM wallets sign a Sign-In with Ethereum message;
Stellar wallets sign a SEP-10 challenge.

Connecting your second wallet links it to the same account, so the app
shows all your trades in one place. The hub shows **Link wallet
required** until you do.

You stay signed in while you use the app. After two weeks without use, or
30 days in all, you sign in again.

<Note>
Each trading wallet also needs a one-time
[settlement key registration](/v3/guides/register-a-settlement-key)
before it can complete trades. The app prompts you when it's needed, or
you can do it up front on the **BLS Keys** page.
</Note>
