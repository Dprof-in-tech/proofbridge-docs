The **Orders** page shows every trade you're a party to, in **Pending
Orders** and **Completed** tabs. Open any order to see its trade page.

## What the order asks of you

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| **Complete Order** | your deposit was never submitted. Click to retry with the same order (no new reservation). |
| **Waiting for Creator** / **Awaiting Lock** | your deposit is in; the maker hasn't locked yet |
| **Lock Tokens** (maker) | a Bridger funded an order on your ad: lock the matching liquidity |
| **Confirming Deposits…** | deposits waiting for enough confirmations |
| **Register BLS Key** | the named wallet has no settlement key yet |
| **Co-sign Settlement** | deposits are confirmed: one click to sign |
| **Waiting for Counterparty** | your signature is in |
| **Aggregating… / Settling…** | the payouts are in progress; nothing to do |
| **Completed** | funds delivered |

## The trade page

- **Status:** whose turn it is, and the steps for the current action
- **Timeline:** every event with its transaction: order reserved, deposit
  landed, maker locked, each co-signature, each proof, each payout, and
  any cancel or dispute
- **Transaction Details:** what you sent, the fee and what you get, the
  **Deadline** and the **Time Left**
- **Your trade half:** your half of the [trade secret](/v3/concepts/trade-secrets),
  with **Copy** and **Download**. It shows whether it matches the
  nullifier on record; if it doesn't, don't co-sign.

## When a trade doesn't settle

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| **Deadline passed** | anyone can now start a cancel |
| **Cancel claimed** | the cancel window is open |
| **Refund on its way** | the cancel finished; your deposit is being refunded |
| **Cancelled** | the trade ended without settling; funds returned |
| **Held: the maker has paused settlement** | the maker halted settlement; it continues when they resume, or the trade takes the cancel path |
| **Disputed** | a dispute was filed |

See [Cancel or get a refund](/v3/guides/cancel-or-get-a-refund) and
[File a dispute](/v3/guides/file-a-dispute).

## Notifications

The bell tracks: a new order on your ad, an order locked, a Bridger
claimed, an order expired, a trade cancelled, a dispute opened, answered
or ruled, and agent alerts (killed, policy expiring or expired, guard
rail).
