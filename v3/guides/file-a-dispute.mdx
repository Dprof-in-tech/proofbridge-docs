Either party can file a dispute while the trade is locked or a cancel has
started, until the cancel window ends. The rules are on
[Disputes](/v3/concepts/disputes).

## File

<Steps>
  <Step title="File Dispute">
    On the trade page, click **File Dispute**. Connect the wallet that is
    a party to this trade.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bond">
    The page shows the **Bond Amount** and when it's returned or
    forfeited. You get it back if the dispute ends without finding you at
    fault. You can lose it if the arbiter service finds you at fault, or
    if on-chain proof shows the trade had already settled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the Dispute">
    Under **What happened?**, describe the problem, and add **Supporting
    Files**. The files are uploaded to the evidence store, and their
    fingerprint goes on-chain with the filing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="File this dispute?">
    Review, then approve the bond and file. Two wallet steps: the bond
    approval, then the filing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once filed, the trade shows **Disputed**, and the page shows the challenge
deadline and the outcome as they arrive.

## Answer

If the other party filed, you can answer **once**, before the challenge
closes. Click **View Dispute**, then **Answer Dispute**, and add your
description and files the same way.

## The outcome

The arbiter service rules, or, with no ruling by the challenge deadline,
the dispute ends as a mutual refund. After the ruling's window,
**Finalize dispute** applies the outcome on the ad chain; the other chain
pays out once the result is proven there. The relayer normally sends this
for you.

If your bond is returned, it appears under **Funds waiting for you** on
the trade page.
