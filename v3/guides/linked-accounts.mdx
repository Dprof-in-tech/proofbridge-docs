An [agent account](/v3/concepts/agents) is a contract account your wallet
owns. It holds your ad so an agent can lock orders on it, but it never
holds funds. You manage them on the **Accounts** page.

## Create an agent account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in on the network">
    Sign in with a wallet on that network first. It becomes the account's
    owner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and link">
    Under **Create an agent account**, choose the network and click
    **Create and link**. Your wallet deploys the account and links it to
    you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow it as a maker">
    Your wallet stays the settlement signer for the account's ads. Go to
    **BLS Keys → Maker approvals** and allow the account, so its ads can
    use your key.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Link an existing account

Under **Link an existing account**, sign in with the wallet that owns the
account, enter the account address (`0x…` on Ethereum, `C…` on Stellar)
and click **Link**. The relayer checks on-chain that your wallet is the
owner.

## Unlink

**Unlink** removes the account from your view. Its ads stay on-chain, but
you can't act on them in the app until you link it again.

## Use it

When you [create an ad](/v3/guides/provide-liquidity), choose the account
under **Make the ad from**. The liquidity comes from your wallet and goes
straight into the escrow through the account.
