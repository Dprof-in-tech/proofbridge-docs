Soak runs on testnets, so you need test funds on both sides.

## In-app faucet

The **Faucet** page mints the test tokens the soak routes use (WXLM and PB
on Sepolia, wETH and PB on Stellar) to your connected wallet.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Faucet">
    Each network has its own section. Connect the wallet for that network
    if it shows **Not connected**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim a token">
    Click the token to claim. The faucet mints **1,000,000** of that token
    to your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for it to land">
    The claim shows **Processing**, then **Confirming**, then
    **Confirmed**. It usually takes under a minute, and it's safe to close
    the window: the claim keeps running.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* SCREENSHOT: Faucet page, Sepolia section open, one claim in progress */}
<Screenshot caption="The faucet: one section per network" />

The faucet refuses a claim when:

- your wallet already holds **10,000** or more of that token
- a claim for the same token is still in progress
- the faucet has had repeated failures on that chain; try again later

## Gas

- **Sepolia ETH:** any public Sepolia faucet
- **Testnet XLM:** Stellar Friendbot funds new accounts

<Note>
Key registration gas is paid by the relayer on both chains. You only need
gas for your own deposits, locks and ad transactions.
</Note>
