---
title: "Cancels"
description: "How a trade that misses its deadline ends, and the route that serves every call you can send yourself"
---

A trade that does not settle before its deadline gives everyone their money back. The escrows run
the cancel themselves, in permissionless steps that anyone may send. The relayer sends them for you
and mirrors the progress onto the trade. This page covers what happens on chain, and the one read
route that serves everything you need to send each step from your own wallet. See
[Cancels and refunds](/v3/concepts/cancels-and-refunds) for the concept.

## Endpoints

| Endpoint | Auth | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /v1/trades/:id/cancel` | none | Every self-serve ending for the trade: the calls, their state, and the proofs |

## There is no cancel endpoint

No relayer route cancels, refunds or claims. Every one of those is a permissionless contract call that
takes the order's parameters. The relayer sends them as each becomes possible, and any wallet may send
them too. `GET /v1/trades/:id/cancel` hands out what each call needs, proofs included. The trade page
offers a button for each step that applies, built on this route.

Two settlement routes refuse work once a trade needs the cancel path:

| Route | Status | Message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `POST /v1/trades/:id/cosign` (first signature) | `409` | `order deadline … is inside the …s settlement horizon … — this trade needs the cancel path, not a co-signature` |
| `POST /v1/trades/:id/cosign`, `GET /v1/trades/:id/settlement/pair` | `409` | `trade is cancelled — settlement material is not served` |

## Who is the primary

The **`AdManager` on the ad chain is the primary.** Only there does a clock run, and only there is the
cancel decided. The **`OrderPortal` on the order chain follows.** It has no clock after the deadline:
it refunds the bridger only against a proof of the primary's cancel leaf, and pays the maker against
the primary's settled or forfeit leaf. Nothing on it can refund on time alone, so nothing can get
ahead of the primary's decision.

Those proofs are checked against a root of the ad chain published by the
[anchor service](/v3/concepts/root-anchors). If no root is anchored yet, the refund waits until one is.

## What happens on chain

None of these calls checks who sends it, and none is stopped by a pause. A pause stops only new ads,
funding, locks and deposits, never an exit. EVM names first, Stellar names in brackets.

| Call | Escrow | Accepted when | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `claimCancel` (`claim_cancel`) | `AdManager` | Order `Open` and the deadline has passed | Opens the cancel window (status `Claimed`). No funds move |
| `finalizeCancel` (`finalize_cancel`) | `AdManager` | Order `Claimed` and the window has ended | Returns the maker's lock to the ad's free liquidity (not to the maker's wallet), status `Cancelled`, records the cancel leaf |
| `cancelNeverLocked` (`cancel_never_locked`) | `AdManager` | The maker never locked and the deadline has passed; takes a proof of the bridger's deposit | Status `Cancelled`, records the cancel leaf. No funds move |
| `refundByCancel` (`refund_by_cancel`) | `OrderPortal` | Order `Open`, with a proof of the cancel leaf under an anchored root | Refunds the bridger's deposit to `params.bridger` |
| `payMakerByForfeit` (`pay_maker_by_forfeit`) | `OrderPortal` | Order `Open`, with a proof of the primary's forfeit leaf | Pays the bridger's deposit to the maker |
| `recordSettled` (`record_settled`) | either | That leg has paid, once | Records the leg's settled leaf so the other leg can be proven paid |
| `presentSettled` (`present_settled`) | either | Order `Open`, `Claimed` or `Disputed`, with a proof of the other leg's settled leaf | Settles this leg instead of cancelling it. Evidence always beats the clock |

The dispute calls (`finalizeDispute`) are on [Disputes](/v3/api/disputes).

A few things a client needs to know:

- **Payouts keep working into the window, but not equally.** The `AdManager` payout to the bridger is
  accepted until just before the window ends. The `OrderPortal` payout to the maker stops shortly
  before the deadline. So past the deadline the maker cannot settle by co-signature, but the bridger
  still can until the window closes.
- **The window's end is a view.** `cancelFinalizesAt(params)` (`cancel_finalizes_at`) returns it, grace
  included, or `0` when no window is open. The relayer copies it into the trade's `claimFinalizeAt`.
  When the maker halted settlement on this order, the window gets an extra grace period. See
  [Deadlines and clocks](/v3/concepts/deadlines-and-clocks).
- **A payout that fails to push is credited, not lost.** The amount waits in
  `claimable(recipient, token)` and the recipient pulls it with `claim(recipient, token)`, which works
  during a pause too.

## Every self-serve ending

`GET /v1/trades/:id/cancel` lists every call a party can send to end the trade: the cancels and
refund, the forfeit payout, and the settled evidence. For each it gives the chain and escrow, the
order's parameters, when the chain accepts it, and, for the calls that take a proof, the proof the
relayer built.

It needs no auth, although the trade itself is party-only. It is the refund's liveness path: a party
who lost its session, or anyone it asks for help, can still send the refund. Every field is public,
every call is permissionless, and a refund always pays `params.bridger`, whoever sends it. The route
has a strict per-IP rate limit.

### Paths

| `path` | When | `steps` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `locked` | The maker's lock is on the ad chain | `claimCancel`, `finalizeCancel`, `refundByCancel` |
| `never-locked` | The deposit is on the order chain and the maker never locked | `cancelNeverLocked` (with its proof), `refundByCancel` |
| `dispute` | A dispute is open, or ended without a forfeit | `refundByCancel` only; the dispute's own steps are on [Disputes](/v3/api/disputes) |
| `forfeit` | A forfeit leaf is on the ad chain | `payMakerByForfeit` (with its proof): the deposit goes to the maker |
| `settled` | One leg has paid | While the other has not: `recordSettled` on the paid leg, then `presentSettled` (with its proof) on the other. None once both have paid, or when the unpaid leg already ended |
| `none` | The deposit is not on chain (on EVM, not yet `EVM_CONFIRMATIONS` deep) | None, and `params` is `null` |

### Step states

| `state` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `waiting` | Not yet; `waitingFor` says why |
| `ready` | The chain should accept it now and everything it needs is served |
| `done` | Its effect is on chain, whoever sent it |

| `waitingFor` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `deadline` | The deadline has not passed |
| `claim` | No cancel window is open yet |
| `window` | The window is open; wait for `claimFinalizeAt` |
| `cancel-leaf` | The ad chain has not cancelled yet |
| `settled-leaf` | The paid leg's settled leaf is not recorded yet: send `recordSettled` |
| `proof` | The relayer has not built the proof yet |

Step states come from the two escrows, so a call you sent yourself shows `done` on the next read.
Chain reads are shared between callers for 15 seconds. If a read fails, the states fall back to the
relayer's records and `stateFrom` is `records-fallback`: a step may then show `ready` although it is
done. Show that as "status unconfirmed". Sending a call too early, or twice, only reverts.

### Sending each call

The escrow is `contractAddress` on `chainId`. On Stellar, the 32-byte fields are `BytesN<32>`.

| `call` | EVM | Stellar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `claimCancel` | `claimCancel(params)` | `claim_cancel(params)` |
| `finalizeCancel` | `finalizeCancel(params)` | `finalize_cancel(params)` |
| `cancelNeverLocked` | `cancelNeverLocked(params, targetRoot, nullifierHash, proof)` | `cancel_never_locked(params, target_root, nullifier_hash, proof)` |
| `refundByCancel` | `refundByCancel(params, targetRoot, proof)` | `refund_by_cancel(params, target_root, proof)` |
| `payMakerByForfeit` | `payMakerByForfeit(params, targetRoot, proof)` | `pay_maker_by_forfeit(params, target_root, proof)` |
| `recordSettled` | `recordSettled(params)` | `record_settled(params)` |
| `presentSettled` | `presentSettled(params, targetRoot, proof)` | `present_settled(params, target_root, proof)` |

`targetRoot`, `nullifierHash` and `proof` come from the step's `package`.

### Keep the packages

The proofs need nothing secret: each proves a leaf already on the other chain, against a root the
target chain has already anchored. Only the relayer builds them, and the route serves a package as
soon as the chain allows the call and the relayer has proved it. A package does not expire, so
download each one when it appears and keep it with the trade: you can still send it if the relayer's
API is unreachable later. `claimCancel` and `finalizeCancel` need no proof and work from the saved
`params` alone. Packages are removed 24 hours after the trade ends.

## What to poll

Poll [`GET /v1/trades/:id`](/v3/api/trades#one-trade) for the trade's progress through the cancel,
and `GET /v1/trades/:id/cancel` when you want to send a step yourself.

| Situation | `status` | Other fields | Show |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before the deadline | `LOCKED` (or `ACTIVE`) | `deadline` in the future | The normal settlement screens |
| Deadline passed, not yet claimed | `LOCKED` / `ACTIVE` | `claimFinalizeAt` null | "Expired, cancelling" |
| Window open | `CLAIMED` | `claimFinalizeAt` set | "Cancel window ends at …". A settlement inside it still wins |
| Cancel finalized, refund pending | `CLAIMED` (`ACTIVE` for a never-locked order) | Past `claimFinalizeAt` (or past `deadline`) | "Refund on its way" |
| A settlement beat the claim | `CLAIMED` | `claimFinalizeAt` null | Settling; turns `COMPLETED` once the other leg follows |
| Done | `CANCELLED` | `cancelReason` set | The reason. For the maker: the lock is back in the ad, withdraw it from there |
| Dispute open | `DISPUTED` | `disputeChallengeDeadline`, `disputeFinalizeAt`, `disputeOutcome` | See [Disputes](/v3/api/disputes) |

The trade never moves backwards. Once the `AdManager` has cancelled and the refund is still to land,
the trade stays `CLAIMED` with the window's end in `claimFinalizeAt` until the refund lands and it
becomes `CANCELLED`. The `cancelReason` values are listed on [Trades](/v3/api/trades#trade-statuses).

[`GET /v1/trades/:id/settlement`](/v3/api/settlement#settlement-status) follows along: `Claimed`
while the trade is `CLAIMED`, `Disputed` while `DISPUTED`, and `Cancelled` with
`signaturesCollected: 0` once it is `CANCELLED`. Both parties also get a `TRADE_CANCELLED`
notification, with text chosen by the reason.
