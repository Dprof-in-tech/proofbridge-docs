Every trade event on a chain is appended as a leaf to that chain's
**Merkle Mountain Range (MMR)**, an append-only tree kept by the
`MerkleManager` contract. Its root commits to every leaf ever added, and
every cross-chain proof is built against it. Only the escrow contracts
can append. The fundamentals are unchanged from v1 (append-only,
Poseidon2-hashed); this page covers what v3 records.

## Leaves with a domain

A leaf commits to the order hash and a **domain**, the kind of event:

```
leaf = poseidon2(field_mod(orderHash), domain)
```

v2 recorded deposits only. v3 also records cancels, settled payouts and
forfeits, so the other chain can act on how a trade ended. The full list
is on [Deposit proofs](/v3/concepts/deposit-proofs#one-circuit-several-events).
The domain says where the leaf is used: a lock on the ad chain is used
by the payout on the order chain, and a deposit on the order chain by the
payout on the ad chain. A proof about one event can never be bent to
serve another.

## Roots are what you co-sign

Both chains' MMR roots are fields of the
[SettlementAuth](/v3/concepts/co-signing) each party signs. Your
signature authorizes settlement against a *specific, known* state of both
trees. The cancel and forfeit paths use roots published by the
[anchor service](/v3/concepts/root-anchors) instead.

## The relayer's copy is rebuildable

The relayer keeps a local copy of each chain's tree to build proofs
quickly. The copy is a **projection of on-chain events**, not a source of
truth: it replays the chains' append events and checks its root against
the on-chain root at every step. Wiped, it rebuilds from the chains
alone.

## Build on it

The [`proofbridge-mmr`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/proofbridge-mmr)
npm package produces byte-identical outputs to the on-chain contracts.
Use it to rebuild a tree from chain events, generate inclusion proofs
offline, or check settlement state independently.
