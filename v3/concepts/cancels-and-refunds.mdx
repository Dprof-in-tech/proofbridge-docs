---
title: "Cancels and refunds"
description: "How a trade that doesn't settle ends: cancel, refund, and the never-locked case"
---

A trade that doesn't settle by its [deadline](/v3/concepts/deadlines-and-clocks)
still ends, and each side gets its funds back. No fee is taken on any of
these paths.

## Before any money moves

Creating an order **reserves** part of the Maker's ad. If the Bridger
never funds it, the reservation is released after ~30 minutes. Nothing
happened on-chain, so there's nothing to refund.

## A locked trade that didn't settle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the cancel">
    After the deadline, anyone can start a cancel on the ad chain
    (`claimCancel`). The trade shows **Cancellation claimed** and the
    cancel window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The cancel window">
    During the window, a co-signed payout or a proof that the trade
    settled still ends it as settled. Either party can also file a
    [dispute](/v3/concepts/disputes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the cancel">
    After the window, anyone can finish it (`finalizeCancel`). The Maker's
    lock returns to the ad, and a cancel leaf is recorded in the ad
    chain's Merkle Mountain Range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refund the deposit">
    On the order chain, the refund (`refundByCancel`) proves that cancel
    leaf and returns the Bridger's full deposit. The trade shows **Refund
    on its way**, then **Cancelled**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The order chain checks the cancel against a root published by the
[anchor service](/v3/concepts/root-anchors). If the anchor service is
unavailable, refunds on the order chain wait until it returns: the order
chain has no clock of its own and only follows the ad chain's proven
outcome.

## An order the Maker never locked

If the Bridger funded the order but the Maker never locked, there's no
lock to release. After the deadline, anyone can cancel the order on the ad
chain (`cancelNeverLocked`) by proving the Bridger's deposit. That records
a cancel leaf, and the Bridger is refunded on the order chain the same way
as above.

The same route refunds an order whose [fee](/v3/concepts/fees) changed
between the deposit and the lock.

## Who sends these transactions

All of these calls are permissionless. The relayer normally sends them,
so you don't have to do anything. If you'd rather not wait, the trade page
has a button for each step that applies (**Claim cancel**, **Finalize
cancel**, **Cancel never-locked order**, **Refund the deposit**), and the
relayer serves the data and proofs each one needs.

## A payout that couldn't be delivered

If a payout can't be pushed to a recipient (for example, a token transfer
fails), the amount isn't lost and the trade still ends. It's credited to
the recipient as a claimable balance, which they withdraw with `claim`.
Claiming is never blocked by a pause.

## One side settled, the other didn't

If one side's payout landed but the other's didn't, the missing side can
be completed by proving the settled one (`presentSettled`). The trade page
offers **Record the settled leg** and **Settle the other leg** for this.
