---
title: "Disputes"
description: "Filing a dispute, the bond, evidence, rulings and the four outcomes"
---

A dispute is how either party challenges a trade that went wrong. It's
part of the contracts: filing, the bond, the ruling and the payout all
happen on-chain, on the ad chain. The order chain has no dispute of its
own; it follows the outcome the ad chain records.

## Who can file, and when

Only the trade's two parties, the Maker and the Bridger, can file. They
file on the ad chain (`AdManager.dispute`), while the trade is locked or a
cancel has started, and before the cancel window ends.

Filing does three things: it posts a **bond**, records the filer's
**evidence**, and stops the cancel from completing while the dispute is
open. It pays nobody and decides nothing.

The other party can answer once (`respondToDispute`), with their own
evidence, before the challenge deadline.

## The bond

The bond is a flat amount in the chain's wrapped native token, the same
for every order on the route. On soak it's 0.001 WETH on Sepolia and 1
XLM on Stellar.

## Evidence

Evidence is a set of files uploaded to the evidence store. The filing
records the hash of its manifest on-chain, so the evidence can't be
changed after filing.

**Evidence that the trade settled always wins.** A co-signed payout, or a
proof that the trade settled on the other chain, ends a dispute as
settled at any time while it's open. A dispute can delay a settlement only
until someone presents that proof.

## Rulings

When there's no proof of settlement, the **arbiter service** rules. It
can rule one of three outcomes: **mutual refund**, **bridger forfeit** or
**maker forfeit**. It can't rule that the trade settles; only evidence can
do that.

A ruling doesn't pay out at once. It opens a short window in which proof
of settlement still overrides it. After that, anyone can finalize the
dispute (`finalizeDispute`), and the ruling applies.

If no ruling arrives by the challenge deadline, the dispute ends as a
**mutual refund**. Nothing waits on the arbiter service showing up.

The challenge deadline is the later of the filing time plus the challenge
period (1 hour on soak) and the end of the order's cancel window.

## The four outcomes

| Outcome | How it arises | The Maker | The Bridger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trade settles | evidence only | receives the Bridger's deposit | receives the payout, less the fee |
| Mutual refund | a ruling, or no ruling by the deadline | the lock returns to the ad | the deposit is refunded |
| Bridger forfeit | a ruling | the lock returns to the ad, and the Maker also receives the Bridger's deposit | nothing |
| Maker forfeit | a ruling | loses the locked amount | receives the locked amount less the protocol fee, and the deposit is refunded |

The ad chain records each outcome as a leaf the order chain acts on: a
settled leaf, a cancel leaf for a mutual refund or maker forfeit, or a
forfeit leaf for a bridger forfeit.

## Where the bond goes

The bond returns to the filer when the outcome was on their side:

| How the dispute ends | The bond |
| --- | --- |
| Mutual refund | returned to the filer |
| A forfeit against the party the filer accused | returned to the filer |
| A forfeit against the filer | to the protocol fee pool |
| Evidence shows the trade settles | to the protocol fee pool, since the trade was provably fine |

One exception: a Bridger who filed because the Maker blocked a co-signed
payout gets the bond back even if the trade then settles.

## What the relayer does

The relayer has no dispute authority. When a dispute is filed, it first
checks whether the trade settled on the other chain, and if so presents
that proof. Otherwise it prepares a case file for the arbiter service.
Its only writes are the permissionless calls anyone could send, such as
`finalizeDispute` and `presentSettled`.
